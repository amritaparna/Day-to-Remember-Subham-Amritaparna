Amritaparna & Subham To Exchange Engagement Rings

After 3 Years 11 Months & 11 Days of shared laughter, cherished memories, and boundless companionship, a formal invitation is extended to all loved ones.

A long-awaited milestone approaches for the Pal and Samanta households as the families prepare to host the official engagement ceremony of Amritaparna Pal and Subham Samanta. Scheduled for Friday, October 23, 2026, the grand celebration will take place at Subhangan Marriage Hall in Madhyamgram, Kolkata.

The upcoming union marks the next chapter in a romance that blossomed nearly four years ago. Over the past three years and eleven months, Amritaparna and Subham have cultivated a relationship built on a foundation of shared dreams, mutual respect, and deep understanding—a bond continuously supported by the warmth and blessings of their loved ones.

"We are thrilled to embark on this lifelong commitment," the couple shared ahead of the festivities. "Having our cherished family, mentors, and friends by our side as we mark this milestone makes the day truly complete."

Organizers promise an enchanting evening filled with rich traditional ceremonies, heartwarming toasts, an ambient musical atmosphere, and a lavish celebratory feast designed to honor the couple's journey as esteemed guests gather to witness their formal engagement.

"Your presence, love, and warm blessings are the greatest gifts as we step into this new chapter together."
